Chapter 3: Chemical Danger

Chapter 4: Team Protocol

3 Golden Rules If You Spot a Spicy Pillow

1. Never Press or Puncture

Never press the trackpad down, clamp it, or pierce the foil. Even a tiny prick sparks violent ignition.

2. Unplug & Quarantine

Unplug charger immediately. Move laptop to a hard, non-flammable surface (metal tray, stone counter, tile).

3. Submit IT Swap Ticket

Request an emergency hardware swap. Must go to hazardous e-waste disposal, never regular trash.
