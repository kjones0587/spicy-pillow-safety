Emergency Response Protocol

What If Active Thermal Runaway Occurs?

If you hear hissing/whistling, smell sweet chemicals, or see white smoke popping from the chassis:

1. ISOLATE FROM FLAMMABLES

If sitting near curtains, bedding, or loose papers, immediately slide or move it onto a granite countertop, tile floor, or metal baking sheet.

⚠️ Use oven mitts / tongs if hot!

2. EVACUATE FUMES & NO WATER

Do not inhale the white smoke (toxic Hydrogen Fluoride gas). Never throw cups of water on it—causes explosive steam flare-ups.

⚠️ Toxic gas & steam hazard!

3. CLOSE DOOR & CALL 911

Close the room door behind you to seal off toxic smoke and flame spread. Evacuate people & pets, and call 911 immediately.

⚠️ Isolate the fire zone!
