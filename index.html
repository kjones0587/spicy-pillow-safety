Act 4: Team Operating Procedure

3 Golden Rules If You Spot a Spicy Pillow

1. Never Press or Puncture

Never force the trackpad back down, clamp the shell, or puncture the foil. Even a tiny prick brings oxygen and sparks immediate fire.

2. Unplug & Quarantine

Unplug charger immediately and power down. Move device to a hard, non-flammable surface (tile floor, granite counter, or metal baking sheet).

3. Submit IT Swap Ticket

Request an emergency hardware swap. Swollen batteries must go to certified hazardous e-waste facilities, never regular trash.
